Refund and Cancellation Policy

ErMate is operated by Varah Health and Technology Private Limited (“we”, “us”). Version 2026-10-21. Questions: support@ermate.in

What you buy

ErMate sells prepaid digital credits and a one-time Individual Pro period (30 days). There is no subscription auto-debit, so there is nothing to cancel.

When we refund

  • Duplicate or failed payments: if money was debited but credits or the plan were not added, or you were charged twice, we refund in full.
  • Change of mind within 7 days of purchase: we refund the unused part of that purchase. Credits already used from it are not refunded.
  • Service fault: if an AI feature fails after consuming credits, write to us and we will restore or refund them.

When we do not refund

Credits already used, free starter credits, and purchases older than 7 days (except duplicate or failed payments).

How it works

Email support@ermate.in from your registered email with the payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and normally reach you in 5–7 working days. When a refund is processed, the matching credits (and any Individual Pro days) are removed from your account in proportion to the amount refunded.
