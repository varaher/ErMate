Privacy Policy

ErMate is operated by Varah Health and Technology Private Limited (“we”, “us”). Version 2026-10-21. Questions: support@ermate.in

1. What ErMate is

ErMate is a documentation and clinical-support tool for emergency doctors. You, the doctor, decide what to enter. ErMate is not a patient-facing service.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, role, workplace, department and settings you provide at signup.
  • Clinical content you enter: case sheets, dictations, pasted notes, handovers, discharge summaries and logbook entries.
  • Voice and images: audio you record and photos or documents you scan, only when you use those features.
  • Usage and billing: AI credit usage, plan, and payment references (order and payment IDs). We do not see or store your card or UPI details; payments are handled by Razorpay.

3. How we use it

To provide the app, save your work, run the AI features you request, bill you, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

We use trusted processors to run ErMate: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, hosting), Sarvam AI (speech-to-text), Anthropic (AI language model), Google Gemini (reading images and documents), and Razorpay (payments). Each receives only what is needed for its function. Before clinical text is sent to an AI language model, ErMate applies automatic de-identification of patient identifiers. Please still avoid entering identifiers you do not need.

5. Patient information

You are responsible for entering patient information lawfully and in line with your hospital’s policies. Cases in a hospital workspace belong to that hospital’s record and are visible to its verified team according to the roles it assigns. Cases in your individual workspace are private to you.

6. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account in the app: More → Delete my account.
  • Deleting removes your login, profile, individual-workspace cases, dictation sessions, logbook and discussion history.
  • Records held in a hospital workspace remain part of that hospital’s record. Your name may remain on them as the author.
  • Payment and credit ledgers are kept for tax and audit purposes (up to 8 years) and no longer link to a profile.
  • Routine backups and logs are overwritten on their normal cycle.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data and to withdraw consent. Use the in-app options or write to support@ermate.in. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within legal limits.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by account and role, and AI and payment actions are authenticated. No system is perfectly secure; tell us promptly if you suspect misuse.

9. Children

ErMate is for medical professionals aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the version above and ask for fresh consent where required.
